01 / Overview

Project Overview

SmartBudget is a modern personal finance application aiming to make budget tracking seamless. In a world full of complex budgeting spreadsheets and overwhelming finance portals, SmartBudget focuses on simple daily habits, enabling users to log transactions in under 3 seconds and view clean, predictive visual analytics.

The Challenge

Most expense trackers fail because users find them tedious to update. Daily transaction logging requires navigating multiple screens, resulting in high drop-off rates and incomplete financial datasets.

The Approach

Through UX research, we prioritized speed and ease of access. We designed a prominent, one-tap "quick-add" interface and combined it with progress rings and gamified budget milestones that keep users engaged and motivated.

02 / Focus

Problem & Goal

The Problem

Manual entry is the biggest bottleneck in budgeting applications. Users forget to record transactions, lose awareness of category limits, and only realize they have overspent at the end of the month, causing frustration and disengagement.

The Goal

Reduce daily logging time to under 3 seconds. Visualize category budgets dynamically so users understand their safe-to-spend limit at a single glance, ultimately helping them reduce overall monthly overspending by 15-20%.
